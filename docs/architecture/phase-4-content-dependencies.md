## 1. Content Verification Policy & Governing Principles

In order to protect the legal standing, national prestige, and institutional credibility of the **AIC-ADT Baramati Foundation**, all content published on the public platform must satisfy the following non-negotiable governance principles:

1. **Zero Unverified Invention:** No legal registrations, dates, government approvals, leadership titles, startup metrics, funding tranches, farmer numbers, or contact credentials may be fabricated or assumed.
2. **Authoritative Evidence First:** Content is classified as **Verified** only when supported by official government documentation (NITI Aayog, DPIIT, MeitY), formal board minutes, or executed institutional agreements.
3. **Mandatory Placeholder Badging:** Where data points are plausible but lack formal documentation, they must render with explicit audit tags:
   - `[Audited as of FY 2024-25]`
   - `[Verification Pending — AIC-ADT Leadership]`
   - `[Sample Illustrative Data — Subject to Management Sign-Off]`
4. **Data Privacy Protection:** Private venture financial turnover, personal mobile numbers, and unverified individual claims must never be exposed without written founder authorization.

## 3. Mandatory Governance Checklist for Implementation

During page authoring in Phase 4, every engineer must verify the following checklist:
- [ ] No unverified numbers appear without an audit date badge (`[Audited as of FY 2024-25]`).
- [ ] No private financial turnover figures appear without written founder authorization.
- [ ] No external insecure media URLs (e.g. `http://test.aic-sku.com/...`) are referenced.
- [ ] All PDF downloads are hosted locally under `/downloads/...` and serve with valid MIME types.
- [ ] All Marathi translations preserve semantic accuracy and use proper Devanagari Unicode.
