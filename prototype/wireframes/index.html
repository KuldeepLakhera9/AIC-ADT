[D] Institutional Introduction & Mission

Grounded in Baramati, Scaling Globally

Combining the 50-year grassroots agricultural heritage of the Agricultural Development Trust with national venture building mandates.

1. Lab-to-Land Science
Direct validation on a 110-acre live demonstration farm and Krishi Vigyan Kendra network.
2. Seed Funding Linkages
Facilitation of SISFS grants (up to ₹20L), MeitY GENESIS, and angel investor syndicates.
3. Specialized Facilities
In-house FabLab (3D printing, CNC, PCB), NABL testing laboratory, and food processing plant.
