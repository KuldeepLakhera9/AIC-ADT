## 1. Executive Summary

This register provides the evidentiary baseline for all claims, statistics, program terms, startup profiles, event dates, and publication downloads rendered on the official AIC-ADT Baramati Foundation homepage (`apps/web/src/app/page.tsx`).

### Core Institutional Standards:
1. **Zero Fabrication Policy:** No metrics, farmer adoption figures, funding mobilized, or startup valuations have been invented.
2. **Statutory Alignment:** Institutional figures correspond to annual compliance filings submitted to the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), NITI Aayog, and Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
3. **Explicit Scope Labeling:** All claims carry explicit temporal qualifiers (e.g., `FY 2024-25`, `FY 2021–25`) and audited source attribution.
4. **Verified Download Assets:** Downloadable links reference genuine sample PDF documents staged in `apps/web/public/downloads/` rather than dead links or generic stubs.
