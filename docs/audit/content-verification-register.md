# Institutional Content Verification Register

**Target Entity:** AIC-ADT Baramati Foundation  
**Audited Domain:** [https://aicadtbaramatifoundation.org/](https://aicadtbaramatifoundation.org/)  
**Document Purpose:** Baseline inventory of public claims, data figures, institutional affiliations, contact credentials, and governance records requiring formal verification, cross-referencing, and management approval prior to platform migration.  
**Standard Status Definitions:**
- **Verified by authoritative evidence:** Cross-referenced and supported by official government documentation, institutional records, or primary public disclosures.
- **Conflicting sources:** Multiple conflicting values or claims observed across different pages on the existing website or external records.
- **Needs approval:** Plausible or historical data item that lacks explicit documented proof and requires administrative sign-off by AIC-ADT leadership.
- **Not found:** Claimed feature, document, or credential referenced in page copy but completely absent from the website or public records.
