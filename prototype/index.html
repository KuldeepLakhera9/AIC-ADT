About AIC-ADT Baramati Foundation

Bridging 50 Years of Agricultural Heritage with Deep Science

Established in Baramati, Maharashtra, the Atal Incubation Centre - Agricultural Development Trust (AIC-ADT) serves as a flagship ecosystem catalyzing technology-driven venture incubation.

National Mandate

Supported by the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), NITI Aayog, Government of India, to nurture startups in smart farming, biotechnology, food tech, and rural entrepreneurship.

110-Acre Living Lab

Startups gain immediate validation on live polyhouses, drone testing corridors, automated irrigation grids, and an active Krishi Vigyan Kendra network.

NABL-Accredited Rigor

State-of-the-art analytical testing facilities providing precision GC-MS, HPLC, and spectrophotometry for soil, water, pesticide, and food product formulation.

Campus Infrastructure & Prototyping Facilities

Our campus houses five specialized technical facilities: the FabLab Prototyping Workshop (laser cutting, 3D printing, PCB milling), Food Innovation Pilot Plant (retort pouches, extrusion lines, drying), Agri-Media Studio, Analytical Testing Lab, and High-Speed Co-Working Suites.

Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

Official incubation and seed capital facilitation under Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Government of India.

Funding Tranches & Support Scope

  • Proof of Concept Grant: Up to ₹20,00,000 for prototype development, lab trials, and validation.
  • Commercialization Debt: Up to ₹50,00,000 via convertible debentures for market launch and working capital.
  • Analytical Lab Access: Free testing quotas in the NABL-accredited analytical laboratory in Baramati.
  • Intellectual Property: Subsidized patent filing, trademark registration, and legal counsel.

Home / Innovation Focus Areas
Technology Verticals

6 Thematic Innovation Focus Areas

Developing cutting-edge solutions for climate-resilient agriculture, precision agronomy, and rural value chains.

1. Agri-Tech & Robotics

Autonomous farm implements, drone spraying calibration, selective harvesting mechanisms, and smallholder mechanization.

2. Smart Farming & Precision

IoT soil telemetry probes, micro-weather stations, variable-rate fertigation, and satellite crop health index monitoring.

3. Biotechnology & Biofertilizers

Beneficial soil microbes, tissue culture propagation, saline soil bio-remediation, and bio-pesticide formulations.

4. Food Processing & Packaging

Millet-based extrusion lines, retort pouch thermal processing, solar cold storage, and non-destructive fruit grading.

5. Climate Tech & Biochar

Agricultural residue pyrolysis, carbon sequestration measurement, solar-powered farm machinery, and circular bioeconomy.

6. Digital Agriculture & AI

Edge-AI computer vision for pest detection, vernacular voice LLMs for agronomy advisories, and predictive yield analytics.
